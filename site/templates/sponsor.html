If a trial is listed wrongly, open a correction request. A dispute adds a note to the trial and never removes it from the data; every outcome is logged in CORRECTIONS.md. For legal compliance rather than the WHO standard, see the FDAAA TrialsTracker and the EU TrialsTracker.
