"No results submitted" means no summary results on ClinicalTrials.gov as of $data_date; journal publications are not searched, so a trial in that row may have reported elsewhere. Legal duty: not determined. The WHO standard is an ethical norm; missing it is not by itself a breach of any law.

"Status overdue" holds trials still marked open more than 395 days after their primary completion date; "inconsistent record" holds trials whose registry entry cannot be read as finished or ongoing, such as a finished trial without an actual primary completion date. Neither is counted as unreported, and neither is "submitted but returned in quality control". These figures are aggregates; no sponsor is named.
