Evidence basis: registry only. "No results submitted" means no summary results on ClinicalTrials.gov as of $data_date; journal publications do not enter these counts, so a trial in that row may have reported elsewhere. The WHO standard is an ethical norm; missing it does not by itself mean any law applies or was broken. Legal duty is a separate axis below.

"Status overdue" holds trials still marked open more than 395 days after their primary completion date; "inconsistent record" holds trials whose registry entry cannot be read as finished or ongoing, such as a finished trial without an actual primary completion date. Neither is counted as unreported, and neither is "submitted but returned in quality control". These figures are aggregates; no sponsor is named.

Legal duty: a separate axis

Whether a law requires results is judged apart from the WHO categories above and never changes them. For the United States we infer a probable applicable clinical trial (ACT) under FDAAA 801 from registry fields, as of $data_date (rules on the methodology page). "Appears overdue" is an inference: a lawful extension may exist that the registry does not publish.
